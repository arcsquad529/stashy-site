Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

Stashy is a personal bookmarking and visual second-brain application that helps you save, organize, and search links, reels, ideas, and notes. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information.

Collecting and Using Your Data

  • Saved Content: When you use Stashy, we store the links, notes, tags, and categories you save so that you can view and organize them across your devices.
  • Account Information: If you choose to sign in with Google, we only receive your basic public profile information (name, email address, and avatar). If you use Stashy as a guest, data is tied only to an anonymous ID on your device.
  • Device Permissions: Stashy may request access to your photo library solely when you choose to save or attach a screenshot. We do not access, scan, or upload any other photos on your device.
  • No Selling of Data: We do not sell, rent, or share any of your personal data or saved bookmarks with third-party advertisers or data brokers.

Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely using industry-standard encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). Access to your saved bookmarks is protected by strict database security rules ensuring only your account can access your library.

Data Deletion & Your Rights

You have complete control over your content. You can edit or delete any saved bookmark, note, or category at any time directly within the app. You may also request account and data deletion by contacting us.

Children's Privacy

Stashy is not directed toward children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
